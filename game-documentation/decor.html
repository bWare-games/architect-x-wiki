Decorations

Decorations is a tab containing multiple objects that can be placed around your site. Objects in this tab serve no functional purpose and are purely cosmetic.

Currently, there are 26 decorations, with 13 having their own additional styles.

Additional styles are denoted by the gear icon.

 

The following category is information about some decorations, in, no specific order.

 

Sign;

The “Sign” decoration has 2 variants. (styles). One being a chrome, metal sheet, and the other being a black plastic sheet with two red lines. Fun fact, both variants of the sign can only hold exactly 22 characters. If exceeded, the excess characters will not be shown. The following photos will show the decoration in-person.

Sofa;

The “Sofa” decoration has 5 variants. (styles). The Sofa looks like a leathered seating made for 3 people to sit on it. The following photos will show the decorations in-person.

Sofas can be used by NPC staffs to regain energy, up to 3 NPCs can be seated per sofa at the same time.

Sofa Couch;

The “Sofa Couch” decoration has 5 variants. (styles). The sofa couch looks like a leathered seating made for 1 person to sit on it. The following photos will show the decorations in-person.

Sofa couch can be used by NPC staffs to regain energy, up to 1 NPCs can be seated per sofa a time.
