# Boosts
In SCP Architect X, you are provided with bombs and passes by dailies or buying from the store.

Bombs that are in the game are:
- Time Bombs: Will speed-up the foundation's loop (such as research, taking breaks, etc)

- Cash bombs: Provide a multiplier to the cash obtained by research
