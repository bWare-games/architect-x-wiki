Structures (Skins)

Structures in SCP Architect X are used to create your facility. These are the walls, windows, doors, floors, roofs, and lights! 

Currently, there are 14 skins available for use in SCP Architect X.

  1. Architect
  2. Office
  3. Maintenance
  4. Neo Core
  5. Legacy Heavy Zone
  6. Heavy Zone
  7. Bunker
  8. Entrance Zone
  9. Elevated Build Skin
  10. Echo Skin
  11. Medium Zone
  12. Warehouse
  13. Facility
  14. Wood
     

Architect 

The Architect skin is the default skin available to everyone. It's minimalistic qualities make this a perfect skin that fits most use cases. 

This skin contains sub-variants, such as half-walls and hanging lights.

 

Office

The Office skin is, as the name suggests, an office-themed structure. Despite it's name, this pack is very robust and it's brick texture allows it to fit into both containment use and personnel use. 

This skin contains sub-variants, such as half-walls and hanging lights.

Maintenance 

The Maintenance skin is an industrial-themed structure that features tubes and electrical wires strung across it's walls. It's intended for maintenance-shaft and other similar zones, but is universal enough to be used in a plethora of ways and ideas.

This skin contains sub-variants, such as half-walls and walls with integrated lights.

Neo Core

The Neo Core skin is a futuristic and neon themed structure that features intricate details such as textured walls, neon lights, piping, unique floors, and unique rooves. This is the first skin to require purchase via Robux, which is deserving because it is also undoubtedly the most detailed skin in the game.

Legacy Heavy

The Legacy Heavy skin is the first purchasable skin that doesn't cost Robux. Instead of Robux, it costs 500,000 cash. Every wall, window, and door includes an hourglass-type shape where the width is bigger on the top and bottom and skinny in the middle. The windows and walls include a vent-type object on the bottom. The door contains a triangle shape and a button on the side. The lights hang from the ceiling.

This skin contains sub-variants, such as half-walls.

 

 

 

 


 

 

 

Heavy Zone

The Heavy Zone skin is the updated version of Legacy Heavy. The skin is based on the color grey and seems to connect throughout items seamlessly. There are three types of windows, with two full windows and one normal window. One variant of the full window has rounded corners, and the other has sharp corners. The half-wall cuts off at the top of the black line on the normal wall, and the gate is simply the door twice.

This skin contains sub-variants, such as half-walls and different windows.

 

 

 

 

 

 

Bunker

The Bunker skin is a skin that is primarily based on a lockdown room setting, with heavy concrete being the main material used. The skin is a retexture of the “Heavy Zone” skin with some slight modifications, like no lines on the walls and a different roof.

This skin contains sub-variants, such as half-walls and different windows.

 

 

 

 

 


 

Entrance Zone

The Entrance Zone skin is perfect for, as the name suggests, the entrance of the site. This skin perfectly mimics the entrance zone of the famous SCP game, “SCP: Containment Breach.” It has an orange and white theme, with the gate having a darker shade of orange than the other objects in the pack. This skin is perfect for a great entrance to your site.

 

 

 

 

 

 


Elevated

The Elevated skin is a very well made futuristic themed structure that features precise and small details such as layering walls, neon lights, well-made ridges, and unique doors. This became the second skin to require purchase via Robux, which is definitely deserved as it follows suit with Neo Core, being another well detailed skin.

Echo

The Echo skin is a simplistic, designed structure that features extra wall variants that stand out from the other skin packs. This pack also contains wall strips of yellow and a unique door and gate design, with the door having key card readers on the side. This became the third skin to require purchase via Robux.
 

Medium Zone

The Medium Zone skin features a simplistic design, with a red stripe consistent at the top of each structure (Excluding floors, roofs and the half-wall.) This pack can be used for zones that hold more dangerous anomalies (Hence, Medium Zone). The pack contains a unique door and gate which have a reinforced design. The floor and roof design is identical to the Bunker skin. 

Warehouse

The Warehouse skin features a large yellow stripe at the bottom of each wall, door and window. There is also a vertical striped pattern on the structures mentioned previously. A vent wall variant is also included in this pack. Warehouse also contains circular lights, unlike other build skins. This build skin is good for cargo areas and logistic bays.

Facility

The Facility skin features a bottom made of grey bricks, with the rest being concrete. This skin comes with a vent wall variant. The doors and windows are similar to the ones of the Warehouse skin, with different lights.

Wood

The Wood skin features a bottom made of grey wood, with the rest being concrete. This skin comes with a vent wall variant. The doors and windows are similar to the ones of the Office skin.

 

Additional Structures

Some structures are not tied to one Structure skin, instead you can use them from any structure skin.

Blast Door

The Blast Door must be unlocked through Foundation Research. The Blast Door is composed of grey simple and rough walls, complete with a very large split-door. It's very handy to separate sectors or different Structure Skins. While blast doors are available in every skin/structure pack, it only comes with one look!
