Foundation Management

The Foundation Management menu is where you can change your facility's name, view profit margins, and manage staff and theie needs.

To access Foundation Management, click the SCP Logo icon in the lower taskbar. (or upper on mobile)

Home

Under home, you can change your facility's name. However, you cannot change your facility to have the same name as another save. You can also manage minimum personnel needs. Under ‘’Home'' category is wiping out all builds from sector 1,2,3 or from whole facility.

Profits

The profits section shows you the amount of money you make from each SCP's test and it's profit per minute/hour. This section is very useful in seeing how lucrative your facility is!

Staff

The staff section allows you to hire/fire staff, view current staff, and see their overall metrics. By keeping the bathroom, energy, and hunger metrics high, you promote faster testing and profits. This section is highly useful in optimizing your facility.

Wondering how to increase these metrics? See NPC Metrics.
